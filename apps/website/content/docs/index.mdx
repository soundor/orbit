Soundor Orbit is a delay with three voices. Each voice repeats what you play once,
at its own time and level, and feeds back into itself for longer tails. The
echo map shows the three repeats as dots you can drag.

## Try it

Orbit runs right here. Press **Play** under the plugin to hear a short phrase
followed by silence, and listen for the echoes in the gaps.

<Player />

## Install the VST3

Orbit is a VST3 plugin for 64-bit Linux and Windows. It works in any DAW
that loads VST3 effects, such as Bitwig Studio, Reaper, Ardour, Cubase or
Ableton Live.

<Downloads />

After a rescan, your DAW lists it as Soundor Orbit, under the vendor
Soundor. Put it on a
track or a send, then read [the controls](/docs/controls).

## Updating

Download the new release and replace the old `Soundor Orbit.vst3` with it. Sessions
and presets saved with an earlier version open as they were, within the same
major version; [the changelog](/docs/changelog) says what changed.
