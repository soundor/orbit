The Orbit on this site is the same plugin as the VST3, running in your
browser: the same interface and the same audio processing.

## Audio sources

The bar under the plugin has **Play**, the volume, and a settings button.
In the settings, choose what Orbit hears:

- the demo phrase (the default)
- an audio file from your computer
- your microphone (the browser asks for permission first; use headphones to
  avoid feedback)
- an oscillator
- nothing

The settings also hold the tempo, which Sync follows.

## Embed Orbit in your page

[orbit.soundor.dev/embed/](https://orbit.soundor.dev/embed/) is Orbit alone,
scaled to fit whatever frame it's in, with the bar under it:

```html
<iframe
  src="https://orbit.soundor.dev/embed/"
  title="Orbit, a three-voice stereo delay"
  allow="autoplay; microphone"
  width="800"
  height="640"
></iframe>
```

`allow="autoplay; microphone"` lets it play once the visitor interacts with
it, and use their microphone if they choose it. Orbit keeps its shape: in a
frame of another shape, its background fills the rest.
