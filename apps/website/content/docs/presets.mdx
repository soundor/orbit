---
title: Presets
description: The four presets Orbit comes with, and how presets behave in your DAW.
---

Orbit comes with four presets. Step through them with the arrows beside the
preset name, or choose one from your DAW's program list: Orbit shows the
same four there.

| Preset             | What it sounds like                                                               |
| ------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Dotted satellites  | Orbit's starting point: repeats on dotted notes. Listen for them between phrases. |
| Warm magnetic      | Short, dark repeats with a little tape drift.                                     |
| Drifting space     | Long, moving tails that fill the gaps.                                            |
| Close conversation | A tight, wide double instead of a long tail.                                      |

Loading a preset sets every control, and also releases Hold and turns off
Original. It's a single undoable step, so undo brings back the sound you
had, its name included.

Your changes are saved with the session. After you edit a preset, its name
shows an asterisk; a session your DAW restores with settings that match no
preset shows **Custom**.
