## The echo map

Each colored dot is one echo. Its position is the echo's time (left to
right) and its level (bottom to top), and the curves around it show how its
repeats fade.

- Drag a dot sideways to change its time, up or down to change its level.
  Past the map's edge, it stays at the edge until the pointer comes back.
- Tab to a dot, then use the left and right arrows for time and the up and
  down arrows for level.

Each echo can be from 1/32 of a note to three beats late.

### Sync and Free

**Sync**, at the bottom left, ties the times to your song's tempo: dragging
snaps to 1/32 notes, and the labels read as note values such as 1/8 D (a
dotted eighth). Click it to switch to **Free**, where a beat on the map is
500 ms whatever the tempo, and the times move freely.

## Knobs

| Knob     | What it does                                                                                           |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Feedback | How much of each echo returns into it: from a single repeat to long tails (up to 92%).                 |
| Tone     | How bright the repeats are, from dark (400 Hz) to open (12 kHz). Each repeat darkens more.             |
| Motion   | Drift in the repeats' pitch and timing, as tape does.                                                  |
| Spread   | How wide the echoes are: at full spread, the first is left, the second in the middle, the third right. |
| Mix      | The balance between your sound and the echoes.                                                         |

Drag a knob up or down, scroll over it, or use the arrow keys; hold Shift for
finer steps. Double-click a knob, or press Home, to reset it.

The echoes come from a mono sum of the input and are spread in stereo; your
original sound keeps its own stereo image.

## Hold

**Hold** catches what the three echoes are carrying and loops it. New sound
doesn't enter the loops while Hold is on, but your original keeps playing
over them, and Tone, Spread and Mix still shape them. Moving an echo while
held changes its loop's speed, and its pitch with it. Feedback is ignored
until you release Hold.

## Original and Orbit

The **Original** and **Orbit** buttons at the top right compare your dry
sound with the effect, with a short fade and without changing any settings.
The echoes keep running while you listen to the original.

## Presets

The arrows beside the preset name step through [the four presets](/docs/presets).
An asterisk after the name means you've changed something since loading it.

## Resizing

Drag the plugin window's corner to make Orbit bigger or smaller: it zooms
rather than rearranging, from half to twice its size.

Press **?** at the bottom right for a short guide to the controls.
