What it does

Three echoes you can see

Each repeat is a dot on the echo map. Drag it sideways for timing, up or down for level. Sync snaps the times to your song’s tempo; Free lets them land anywhere.

Hold catches a moment

Hold loops whatever the echoes carry right now. Your playing goes on over it, and moving an echo while held bends the loop’s speed.

Original or Orbit

Switch between the dry sound and the effect without losing your settings. Four presets give you somewhere to start.
